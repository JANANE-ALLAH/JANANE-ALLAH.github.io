## Summary

A hybrid numerical framework for three-dimensional nonlinear structural problems, combining weak and strong formulations in a high-order scheme with a continuation technique. The hybrid approximation couples a strong-form meshless method (weighted least squares) with the finite element method, which removes the usual drawbacks of strong-form meshless approaches near boundaries and under Neumann conditions. 3D simulations are compared with the conventional weak-form finite element solution.

**Keywords:** Finite element method · Weighted least squares · Weak–strong form · 3D FE-meshless model · High-order algorithm
